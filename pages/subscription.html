TIPECO Subscription Rules

  • Payment must be confirmed before a subscription becomes active.
  • Subscription does not automatically approve listings.
  • Every listing remains subject to TIPECO GROUP Owner review and approval.
  • Active post limits depend on the selected plan.
  • Existing listings are not automatically deleted when a subscription expires.
  • Expired listings become inactive and are hidden from the public marketplace.
  • Listings are not automatically reactivated after renewal; the seller chooses which listings to reactivate.
  • Upgrades can take effect immediately after the required verification and activation.
  • Downgrades take effect at the end of the current subscription period.
